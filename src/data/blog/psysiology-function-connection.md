*This is a run-on post containing much of what I have been thinking about this summer while attempting to re-engineer myself to become a better version of how I currently stand.*

## Personal Context: Tension and Perception
For much of my life, I have had trouble with holding inner tension and not breathing properly. While it didn't necessarily impair my development, I noticed a difference in how I processed information compared to others. Specifically, I found myself ignoring:
* **Expressed emotion**
* **Holistic features:** The ability to judge a situation in context (e.g., navigating a city by "feel" rather than just analytical turn-by-turn directions).

## Brain Lateralization
There is a distinct relationship between reading emotion and judging holistic features; both are **right-brain** activities. Lateralization is an observable fact. You can test this by watching yourself in a mirror:
* **Factual Recall (Left Brain):** Eyes typically look left (0-90 degrees from the nose).
* **Imaginary/Emotional Construction (Right Brain):** Eyes typically look right (angle > 90 degrees).

### The Wasp Anecdote: Gradual Consciousness
Consciousness likely emerged gradually, evolving from core structures outward. I observed a wasp navigating my garage:
1.  It found a small opening to scout a nest location.
2.  When I patched the hole, the wasp repeatedly flew between the nesting site and the blocked exit.
3.  This behavior required both **holistic positioning** (knowing where it was) and **navigational steps** (calculating the route). 

Human intelligence is essentially an extension of these same neural circuits, just with significantly more computational power. Some interesting references:
- PubMed Central Study: [here](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1464420/) outlines learning of language and lateralization in humans
- Wikipedia: [here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lateralization_of_brain_function) Wikipedia link for broad overview on lateralization
- Frontiers in Psychology: [here](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00552/full) outlines various ways in which brain lateralization is measured

## The Power of Two (Dichotomy)
The concept of division into two parts is a fundamental biological and physical principle:
* **Biological:** Cell division, reproduction (requiring two parents), and bilateral symmetry.
* **Physics/Math:** Symmetry principles and binary states (like 0 and 1 in computer chips).
* **Pareto Optimal State:** A singular state is often unstable and must be balanced by its opposite extreme.

## Physiological Impact of Tension
When you are physically tense, you are effectively **restricting blood supply** to specific regions of the brain. 
* **Endocrine System:** The Hypothalamus regulates the Pituitary Gland based on inputs from various brain regions.
* **The Chain Reaction:** If the regions controlling the Hypothalamus receive low blood flow due to tension, they cannot compute the necessary signals to regulate hormones at optimal levels.

## Form and Function
**Blood flow is the essence of one's being.** It is governed by lung size and the organization of blood vessels. Interestingly, one might be able to guess the organization of these vessels by physical traits:
* The shape of one's chin.
* The lines on one's hands (which mark the termination points of certain blood vessels).
